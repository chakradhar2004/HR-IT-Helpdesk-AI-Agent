# Enterprise Global HR Policies & Guidelines

Welcome to the Company HR Knowledge Base. This document provides clear, definitive policy guidelines for all employees regarding leave, remote work, reimbursements, and office schedules.

---

## Section 1: Leave and Attendance Policy

### 1.1 Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid Annual Leave per calendar year, accrued proportionally on a monthly basis. Annual leave may be requested through the HR portal with at least 5 business days advance notice for standard leaves and 2 weeks advance notice for leaves exceeding 5 consecutive business days.

### 1.2 Leave Carry-Forward Rules
Employees are encouraged to utilize their paid time off within the calendar year. However, a maximum of up to **10 unused annual leave days** can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any accumulated annual leave beyond the 10-day limit will lapse automatically on December 31st unless an exception is granted in writing by the Department Head and HR Director. Carried-forward days must be utilized within the first six months (by June 30th) of the new calendar year.

### 1.3 Sick and Medical Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid Sick Leave per calendar year. Sick leave is available from day one of employment. For absences exceeding three (3) consecutive business days, a legitimate medical practitioner's certificate must be submitted to HR upon resumption of work. Sick leave days do not carry forward to subsequent years and cannot be encashed.

### 1.4 Parental and Family Care Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, while secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, available following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

## Section 2: Remote and Work-from-Home (WFH) Policy

### 2.1 WFH Eligibility and Probation
Employees are eligible to apply for regular hybrid or remote work arrangements upon successful completion of their **90-day probationary period**. During the initial 90 days of onboarding and orientation, full-time on-site presence is standard unless otherwise explicitly contracted in the offer letter.

### 2.2 Hybrid Schedule Guidelines
Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **2 days per week** with prior written approval from their direct people manager. Core collaboration days are designated on Tuesdays and Thursdays, during which all department members must be physically present in the office for team synchronizations and cross-functional meetings.

### 2.3 Core Working Hours and Availability
All remote employees must maintain active availability on Slack/Teams and email during standard corporate core hours: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time**. Remote workstations must be equipped with secure, reliable internet connectivity that complies with IT security guidelines.

---

## Section 3: Expense Reimbursement and Allowances

### 3.1 Monthly Internet and Phone Subsidy
Employees who are approved for hybrid or remote work are eligible for a monthly communication and broadband reimbursement of up to **$50 per month**. Claims must be submitted via the Concur expense portal with an attached monthly ISP invoice within 30 days of bill issuance.

### 3.2 One-Time Home Office Ergonomic Allowance
New hires and newly eligible hybrid employees can claim a one-time reimbursement of up to **$500** for ergonomic office equipment, including external monitors, ergonomic chairs, and sit-stand desk converters. Purchases must be made within the first 6 months of employment.

### 3.3 Travel and Client Meals
All work-related travel expenses, lodging, and client entertainment require pre-approval by the manager. Standard meal per diem is capped at $75 per day for domestic business trips.

---

## Section 4: Public Holidays and Annual Office Closures

### 4.1 Recognized Public Holidays
The company recognizes 11 statutory public holidays annually, published each December on the internal intranet calendar.

### 4.2 Year-End Winter Break
In addition to statutory holidays, the company observes an annual paid Winter Shutdown from December 24th through January 1st. These shutdown days are granted as supplemental paid company holidays and are not deducted from employee annual leave balances.
